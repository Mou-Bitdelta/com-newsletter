02.  THE BIG STORY

 

Crypto Markets Hit Two-Month Lows Amid Regulatory Concerns

 

Bitcoin and Ethereum fell to their lowest levels in more than two months as uncertainty surrounding US crypto regulation and security concerns weighed on market sentiment. The sell-off followed developments after the US midterm elections and reports of a major security breach involving Binance.

 

What to watch: Regulatory developments in the US, exchange security measures, Bitcoin's ability to stabilise and institutional activity across digital assets.
