02.THE BIG STORY
 

Binance Hack and Stock Sell-Off Push Bitcoin Below $77,000

 

Binance confirmed a hack, paused deposits and withdrawals, and pledged to cover user losses. Together with a stock sell-off, it pushed Bitcoin below $77,000 for the first time in over a month. Total crypto market cap fell 3.7%, and the Fear & Greed Index dropped to 37 (Fear). Hyperliquid’s HYPE was a rare gainer.

 
What to watch:
 

Whether Bitcoin holds near $76,500, the average cost basis of short-term holders, how sentiment settles as the incident develops, and next week’s US jobs report and midterm elections.
