02.THE BIG STORY
 

Bitcoin Slides Below $63,000 as Crypto Sentiment Weakens

 

Crypto markets remained under pressure this week, with Bitcoin trading around the $63,000 mark and Ethereum moving below $1,800. Continued risk aversion and uncertainty around the Federal Reserve's interest rate outlook added to market volatility.

The broader market also saw significant price movements across altcoins, keeping traders focused on key support levels and upcoming economic data.

 

What to watch:

 
  • Bitcoin's ability to maintain support around $62,000.
  • Ethereum's price movement around the $1,800 level.
  • Institutional flows into and out of digital assets.
  • Upcoming US economic releases and their impact on market sentiment.
